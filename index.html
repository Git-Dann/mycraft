How to play

On a phone or tablet

  • Move: left thumb on the joystick (bottom left)
  • Look: drag with your right thumb, at the same time if you like
  • Build: tap where you want a block
  • Break: press and hold a block
  • Fly: tap Fly (or double-tap Jump). Then Up and Down move you, and Land puts you back on your feet
  • Build or break: the switch at the top right changes what a tap does
  • Undo: the arrow at the top right takes back your last build or break

On a computer

  • Move: WASD, Space to jump, Shift to go down
  • Build / break: right click / left click. Z undoes
  • Blocks: 1 to 9, mouse wheel, E for all
  • Fly: F or double-tap Space, then Space to go up and Shift to go down
  • Mouse: Esc frees it so you can click the hotbar and buttons. Click the world to carry on. H shows or hides the controls

Things to try

  • Ride the coaster: pick the Cart and tap the track. Add Track blocks to make it longer. Stack them one block higher to make hills, and lay them in an L to turn a corner. A Loop piece makes a loop the loop.
  • Bunny Meadow: use the Portal Key inside the carrot frame. Make your own frame from Carrot blocks, 4 wide and 5 tall.
  • Feed a bunny: pick the Carrot block, then tap a bunny (right click on a computer). Your first fed bunny becomes your pet and follows you everywhere.
  • Block powers: Cloud is bouncy, Ice is slippery, Mint makes you zoom, Sand falls, Sunshine and Star Block glow at night. Tap a Note Block to play it, tap Fireworks to launch them, tap a Generator to switch it on or off. Each block's power shows when you pick it.
  • Treasure: a golden carrot is hidden in each world. In the Overworld it's buried in the grass far from home, and in Bunny Meadow it's on a cloud. Look closely for a glitter!
  • TNT: tap it to light the fuse, then stand back. Undo puts everything back.
  • Stickers: collect them all in the Sticker book in the menu.
  • No music on iPhone? Tap the screen once, and check the volume buttons.
  • Electrics: a Generator powers Wire. Wire lights Lamps and pushes Pistons.
