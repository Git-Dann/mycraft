How to play

On a phone or tablet

  • Move: thumb on the left side
  • Look: drag on the right side
  • Build: tap where you want a block
  • Break: press and hold a block
  • Fly: double-tap jump, or the Fly button

On a computer

  • Move: WASD, Space to jump, Shift to go down
  • Build / break: right click / left click
  • Blocks: 1 to 9, mouse wheel, E for all
  • Fly: F or double-tap Space

Things to try

  • Ride the coaster: pick the Cart and tap the track. Add Track blocks to make it longer. Stack them one block higher to make hills. A Loop piece makes a loop the loop.
  • Bunny Meadow: use the Portal Key inside the carrot frame. Make your own frame from Carrot blocks, 4 wide and 5 tall.
  • Bunnies follow you when you hold a Carrot.
  • Electrics: a Generator powers Wire. Wire lights Lamps and pushes Pistons.
